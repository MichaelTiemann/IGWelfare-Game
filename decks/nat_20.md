# Confirming the Crit: The "Nat 20" Sub-Roll (`20:01` to `20:20`)

Rolling a **Nat 20** (landing in the **Top 5% of New Zealand wealth**, starting at the `P95: NZD 500k income + NZD 3M net worth` threshold) and then **rolling the d20 a second time** ("confirming the crit," where each face `20:01` through `20:20` represents a **0.25% slice of the national population** inside the Top 5%, from `P95.0` all the way to the `P99.99` NBR Rich List apex) is the ultimate stress test of the model.

Pulling directly from the **10-Tier Earn + Own Incidence Audit** in **Document 1A (Part IV, Section 3 & Section 6B)** and [`data/earn_plus_own_10tiers.csv`](../data/earn_plus_own_10tiers.csv), here is what happens on that second d20 roll:

1. **On the Active Entrepreneur / Growth Investor Track (`z_h`):** **19 out of the 20 faces (`20:01` through `20:19`, all the way up to `NZD 5M income + NZD 100M net worth`, Tier 9) are STILL net welfare positive (`+5.8%` down to `+0.2%` CE)**, and even the final `20:20` ultra-billionaire slot (`NZD 10M + 250M`, Tier 10) is essentially flat (**`-0.6% CE`**) while retaining **94.5% of their fortune (`NZD 236.3M`)**!
2. **On the Portfolio-Average Track (blending active entrepreneurs and passive landlords):** The first **13 faces of the Top 5% (`20:01` through `20:13`, representing `P95.0` through `P98.25`, up to `~NZD 6.0M` net worth)** are net welfare positive or breakeven on average, and even the highest passive property dynasties on faces `20:14` to `20:20` retain **89.4% to 94.9% of their compounded net worth**—a smaller portfolio adjustment than a routine once-a-decade market correction, except this time their children and grandchildren don't emigrate to Sydney.

## Three Takeaways From the "Double-20" Roll (Why This Stuns the Room)

1. **The "19-Out-of-20" Entrepreneur Rule:** If you roll a Nat 20 to enter the Top 5%, and then roll the d20 a second time as an **Active Entrepreneur or Growth Investor (`z_h`)**, **19 out of 20 faces on that second die (`20:01` to `20:19`, up to `NZD 100M` net worth in Tier 9) STILL win outright**, because the `-3.5 pp` PAYE/corporate tax cut, `-60 bps` cheaper expansion credit, and a `+38%` larger thriving domestic talent pool outweigh the land/wealth levy.
2. **Even on a Passive/Blended Portfolio Basis, Two-Thirds of the Top 5% (`20:01` to `20:13`) Win or Break Even:** People often assume "the Top 5%" means multi-hundred-millionaires. In New Zealand (per Stats NZ HES and Max Rashbrooke's wealth brackets), the bottom two-thirds of the Top 5% (`P95.0` to `P98.25`, holding **NZD 3M to NZD 6M** in net worth and earning **NZD 500k to NZD 630k**) are senior clinicians, partners, farmers, and SME founders whose gains from lower income taxes, cheaper mortgages, and retained children/grandchildren keep their **tier-average welfare positive (`+2.1%` down to `0.0%`)**.
3. **The `20:20` "Double Nat-20" Insurance Bargain (Tier 10):** Even if you roll a 20 and then roll *another* 20 (`20:20`, the `NZD 10M income + NZD 250M net worth` NBR Rich List dynasty in Tier 10), your active family enterprise is within half a percent of breakeven (**`-0.6% CE`**) and retains **NZD 236.3M (94.5% of its fortune)**—and even a passive land dynasty retains **NZD 223.5M (89.4%)** and **NZD 7.05M/yr in after-tax cash flow**. Compare that 5.5%–10.6% once-in-a-generation contribution against routine once-a-decade market crashes (like the `-60%` 1987 crash, the `-40%` 2008 GFC, or the `-22% to -27%` 2022–2024 Auckland/Wellington property drop), which incinerate twice as much wealth for nothing while leaving the country poorer and more divided.
