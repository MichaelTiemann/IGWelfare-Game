# Shooting the Moon: The Plutocrat's Crown, Strategic Ambiguity, & Global Market Contagion
**Companion Coopetitive Rules for *Foils & Founders: The Aotearoa Deck* (v2026.11c)**

In *Foils & Founders*, constructive players cooperate across the shared River to lift Aotearoa onto all three coupled hydrofoil surfaces (`SS0 -> SS1 -> SS2`) while competing for the **Mana Laureate** and productive **Enterprise Wealth ($z_h$)**. Yet just as in New Zealand's real political economy—and just as in *Hearts*—a player may instead attempt to **Shoot the Moon** via rentier state capture.

Trying to hoard passive property and cartel wealth ($z_\ell$) casually is a losing strategy: active enterprise builders ($z_h$) remain net welfare positive right up to the NZD 50M IRD High-Wealth median and break even at the NZD 140M NBR Rich List median, whereas un-rotated passive hoards are deflated by Land Value Taxation (`B:08`), Commerce Commission enforcement (`C:08`), and global liquidity shocks. To win as a pure Wealth Hoarder, you must execute a disciplined, comprehensive plan to capture **all four Pillars of State Capture**—before either your fellow players or global capital markets take your leverage apart.

## 2. Strategic Ambiguity & The Smooth Portfolio Rotation (Turns 1–8)

For four decades, New Zealand's tax and credit settings quietly nudged ordinary investors, stretched *whānau*, and impatient founders into opportunistic shortcuts—whether taking coerced shift cash (`T:02`), jumping a regional rollout queue for their own *tamariki* (`T:04`), or parking seed savings in untaxed land (`T:05`). Early accumulation is therefore **strategically ambiguous**:

1. **The Ambiguous First Pillar (Seed Skim):**
   * Placing your **1st Capture Pillar** face-up in your Private Vault costs **0 upfront Lobbying Wealth**, incurs **0 *mana* penalty**, and **cannot be Judo-blocked** by other players. Immediately collect **+2 Wealth**.
   * Holding a single Pillar looks identical whether you are a constructive Finisher building a cash runway for mid-game River launches or a prospective Plutocrat laying your first stone.
2. **The Smooth Portfolio Rotation ($z_\ell \rightarrow z_h$ Pivot):**
   * Mirroring `B:08`'s predictable 10-year portfolio rotation runway, at the start of any turn while you hold **exactly 1 Pillar**, you may voluntarily **liquidate (discard) it** to help fund a constructive River card play—gaining **+2 Wealth** toward that card's cost and **+1 *Mana*** (counting as active *mana* circulation for the 2-turn Use-It-Or-Lose-It rule).
3. **Showing Your Hand (Escalating to Pillar 2+):**
   * Attempting to place a **2nd distinct Pillar** into your Vault permanently locks out voluntary rotation for the rest of the game and opens the **Civic Watchdog Window**.

## 3. Domino Capture Additionality: Compounding Rents & Eroding Watchdogs

Regulatory capture compounds both economically and institutionally: cartel profits fund media fog, distraction fractures civic resistance, and once three pillars are entrenched, the fourth falls with barely a touch.

### A. Phase I Vault Rent & Governance Drag
Count the number of **distinct Capture Pillars ($k$)** in your Vault during Phase I:
* **1 Pillar:** Gain **+1 Wealth**. *(Zero board drag.)*
* **2 Pillars:** Gain **+2 Wealth**. *(Regulatory Drag: deal **-1 card** into the shared River this turn.)*
* **3 Pillars:** Gain **+4 Wealth**. *(Deep Capture: deal **-2 cards** into the River; launching any Hydrofoil Bay costs **+1 extra Wealth** in commissioning friction, and NZ Social Cohesion advances at **half speed**.)*
* **4 Pillars (*The Plutocrat's Crown*):** Gain **+6 Wealth**. *(Legislative Immunity: newly locked Hydrofoil Bays no longer strip Pillars from your Vault.)*

### B. Phase II Lobbying & Eroding Civic Judo-Blocks
To attempt your **2nd, 3rd, or 4th Pillar**, pay **$k$ Wealth upfront** in lobbying capital (where $k \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ is the number of distinct Pillars you *already* hold). Before the card locks, any Steward with `>= 1` *Mana* may pay `1` Wealth to call a **Civic Judo-Block** on `B:00` (the D20):
* **Counter-Card Bonus (`+4`):** If the defending Steward taps an active River/Wayfinder card—or discards a hand card—listed in that Trap/Boss card's `synergy_links`, add **+4** to the D20 roll.
* **Coalition Defense Bonus (`+4`):** A second Steward may spend `1` Wealth or tap a linked card to add another **+4** *(essential when stopping Pillar 4!)*.
* **Domino Erosion Thresholds:**
  * **Stopping Pillar 2 ($1 \rightarrow 2$):** Roll **10+** on D20 *(needs **6+** with a `synergy_link` counter—75% block)*.
  * **Stopping Pillar 3 ($2 \rightarrow 3$):** Roll **13+** on D20 *(needs **9+** with a counter—60% block)*.
  * **Stopping Pillar 4 ($3 \rightarrow 4$):** Roll **17+** on D20 *(needs **13+** with a single counter—40% block, or **9+** if two Stewards unite!)*. *(If `SS2` is already active, institutional resilience grants defenders an automatic **+3**.)*
* **Resolution:**
  * **Blocked:** The Hoarder's upfront lobbying Wealth stays burned in the bank, they forfeit **-2 *Mana***, and the lead Watchdog earns **+1 *Mana***. Discard the blocked Pillar card.
  * **Unblocked:** Place the Pillar in the Vault and collect **+2 Wealth** plus the card's `feral_kickback_wealth`.

### C. One-Time Legislative Reset (Hydrofoil Bay Enactment)
At the exact moment the crew completes **Bay 1 (Front Foil)**, **Bay 2 (Wing-Sail)**, or **Bay 3 (Rear T-Foil)**, every player holding **1, 2, or 3 Pillars** must immediately discard the corresponding Pillar (**Pillar 3**, **Pillar 2**, or **Pillar 1** respectively) from their Vault. A completed **4/4 Vault** is immune.

## 4. "Davos Is Not Our Friend!" — Phase IV Global Market Contagion

As long as New Zealand's capital circulates through regional enterprises and shared infrastructure, Geneva carry-traders and Davos macro-funds barely glance south. Concentrate domestic property and cartel rents behind offshore debt, however, and global markets smell an unhedged carry trade. When global liquidity freezes, Davos is not your friend—you are the collateral.

At the end of every turn in **Phase IV**, roll `B:00` (D20) for **Global Market Contagion**:

1. **Determine the Global Hazard Window:**
   * Base global liquidity shock triggers on a **Natural 1**.
   * Add **+1 to the trigger cutoff** for each Pillar beyond the first held by the *most-captured player* at the table (`2 Pillars -> 1–2`; `3 Pillars -> 1–3`; `4 Pillars -> 1–4`).
   * Add **+1** from Turn 10 onward if the board is still stuck in `SS0` (sovereign credit fragility).
   * Subtract **-1** if the **Rear T-Foil** (`B:14` Debt-Sinking + `B:15`/`E:03`) is locked *(minimum trigger is always a **Natural 1**)*.

2. **Margin Call on Over-Concentrated Vaults:**
   If the D20 roll lands **at or below** the Hazard Window, check every player holding **25+ Wealth** whose Wealth is strictly above the table average:
   * **Leveraged Wannabe (Holding 2 or 3 Pillars):** *Foreign Margin Call!* Offshore creditors liquidate your collateral at fire-sale prices. **Lose 1/3 of your Wealth** (rounded down) **AND discard 1 Pillar** from your Vault (chosen by the player to your left).
   * **Crowned Plutocrat (Holding 4/4 Pillars):** *Sovereign Bond Tantrum!* Even owning Wellington doesn't mean owning Zurich. On a **Natural 1 or 2**, **lose 1/4 of your Wealth AND discard 1 Pillar** unless you immediately pay **6 Wealth** in hard collateral reserves.
   * **Productive Stewards (Holding 0 or 1 Pillar):** **Completely immune** if protected by `B:14` (-60 bps Sovereign Credit Shield), `C:03` (Lifeline Armor), or `E:03` (Firmed Grid) in the River; otherwise pay a minor **-2 Wealth** FX headwind.

## 5. Turn 20 Endgame Reckoning: Crown vs. The Three Unfortunate Fates

Note also the **2-Turn Use-It-Or-Lose-It *Manaakitanga* Rule**: *mana* lives only while it circulates. Any player who goes **2 consecutive turns** with **0 *Mana* staked and 0 *Mana* earned** loses **-1 *Mana*** (resetting their 2-turn clock). Pure hoarders naturally erode to `0` *Mana* by Turn 20.

Before awarding the **Wealth Laureate** at the end of Turn 20, resolve all Vaults:
* **4/4 Capture Pillars Held — *The Plutocrat's Crown*:** Your monopolies and offshore trusts lock permanently. Multiply your Wealth by **1.25x** and ignore all domestic tax and audit penalties below.
* **1–3 Capture Pillars Held — *The Three Unfortunate Fates*:** Without a complete 4-Pillar shield, your un-rotated rentier fortune faces the real-world balance-sheet reckoning (stacking all that apply):
  1. **Wing-Sail Active (*The 1989 Tokyo Bubble Pop*):** Un-rotated speculative dirt deflates under `B:08` LVT — **lose 1/3 of your Wealth**.
  2. **Rear T-Foil Active & You Hold `< 2` *Mana* (*Commerce Commission & IRD High-Wealth Clawback*):** With zero community *manaakitanga* to defend your license to operate, statutory audits claw back illicit cartel rents — **forfeit -4 Wealth per Pillar** remaining in your Vault (or half of tracked kickbacks).
  3. **`SS0` Sinkhole (*King of the Ashes*):** You sabotaged the hydrofoil so Crown Debt hit 185%–200% of GDP, failed to complete your 4/4 Offshore Escape, and watched educated *rangatahi* depart for Sydney (`H:04`) — **halve your remaining domestic Wealth (0.5x)**.
