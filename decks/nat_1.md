# Confirming the Fumble: The "Nat 1" Sub-Roll (`01:01` to `01:20`)

Rolling a **Natural 1** in tabletop RPGs is a "Critical Fail"—you are born into the **Bottom 5% of New Zealand household wealth (`P0.0` to `P5.0`, Tier 1)**, where net worth is zero or negative (overdrafts, payday loans, emergency housing debt) and family income sits at or below the `NZD 50k` material hardship threshold.

If you roll that **Nat 1** and then **roll the d20 a second time** ("confirming the fumble," where each face `01:01` through `01:20` represents a **0.25% slice of the birth cohort** from the deepest acute state-care crisis at `P0.00–0.25` up to the working-poor rental threshold at `P4.75–5.00`), the contrast between the Status Quo (`SS0`) and the IGWelfare Reform (`SS1 -> SS2`) is the moral and fiscal heart of the entire monograph.

Pulling directly from **Document 1A (Part III Population & Fiscal Sankeys, Part IV Section 3, and the Barnardos / MartinJenkins / Dunedin Study anchors)** and [`data/d20_veil_of_ignorance.csv`](../data/d20_veil_of_ignorance.csv), here is what happens on that second d20 roll:

1. **In the Status Quo (`SS0` — Displacement Mode):** **All 20 faces (`01:01` to `01:20`) are trapped in the 100% Unmitigated Hardship Corner (`i_c = 0`).** Because rents in Auckland and Large regional cities consume over 50%–60% of income (or rural fuel/transport eats the budget), parents have zero cash left for early childhood enrichment. Worse, rolling `01:01` to `01:08` lands the child directly in the acute **Exits 1, 2, and 4** pipeline—Oranga Tamariki state care (**NZD 180k/yr**), substantiated abuse in care (**NZD 181k** lifetime fiscal cost + **NZD 800k+** human pain), avoidable hospitalisations, and adult Corrections (**NZD 150k/prisoner-yr**)—imposing an average national burden of **NZD 105,000 per hardship child per year** while condemning the child to a **52%–92% probability of launching into adulthood stunted (`h1–h2`)**.
2. **In the IGWelfare Scenario (`SS0 -> SS1 -> SS2` — Up on Foils):** **Every single face on the second die (`01:01` to `01:20`) is rescued from the corner and achieves a `+23.5%` to `+38.4%` permanent lifetime welfare gain.** By bypassing the household's cash constraint with **NZD 13.0k/yr of universal in-kind First-2000-Days wraparound (`B:01`)**, buffering adolescent shocks at ages 12–14 with **Montessori / *Tuakana–Teina* and Graeme Dingle Foundation mentoring (`B:02`, `C:03`)**, and seeding every 20-year-old with the **universal Youth Capital Grant (`B:03`, ~NZD 62k net worth at launch)**, **19 out of 20 faces (`01:02` to `01:20`) exit stunting overwhelmingly**, and even the most complex acute-needs slice (`01:01`) sees state-care entries fall by over **94%** (from 5,500 down to under 300 nationally).
