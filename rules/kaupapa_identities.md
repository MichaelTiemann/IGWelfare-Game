# Kaupapa Identities: Wayfinder Personas & Dual-Class Synergies

**Companion Persona Deck (`K:01` – `K:07`) for *Beyond the Magic Money Tree — Foils & Founders: The Aotearoa Deck***

In the real economy, no leader is confined to a single bureaucratic box: wayfinders can be teachers or venture capitalists; artists can be community leaders; philanthropists can be quantitative economists; and thriving retirees bring sixty years of lived synthesis to the next generation.

While [**Beyond the Magic Money Tree**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OuKR1aV3zMeuOVGXjkO0Y4lGCbT3VsQepuPpolMUR00/edit?tab=t.0) contains the core game mechanics and expansion decks (`B:01–09`, `T:01–03`, `C:01–06`, `N:01–06`, and `H:01–05`), this companion deck gives every player at the table a **human identity, a shared purpose (*kaupapa*), and cross-sector alliance powers (*kotahitanga*)**.

---

## How the *Kaupapa* Persona Deck Fits into the Game (`P:DD` Card System)

At the start of a workshop or game session, each player selects one **Primary Wayfinder Persona (`K:01` – `K:06`)** to sit in their Command Zone, and may pair it with a **Secondary Calling (Dual-Class Overlay)** or co-tap their Persona with another player at the Big Table:

* **Solo Play (Innate *Kaupapa*):** Once per turn, tap your Persona to trigger its core sector advantage on the main board.  
* **Dual-Class / *Kotahitanga* Synergy:** When you pair two Personas together (either on your own board as a Dual-Class Wayfinder, or by co-tapping with a partner across the table), you unlock the **Shared *Kaupapa* Ability**—bypassing the institutional silos that defeat isolated single-sector players.

## The *Kaupapa* Identity Cards (`K:01` – `K:07`)

### `K:01` — The Catalytic Steward: Philanthropist, Iwi Trust, & Community Leader

* **Type:** Wayfinder Persona — *Manaakitanga*, *Whanaungatanga*, & R\&D Seed (Lens 1\)  
* **Innate *Kaupapa* (Tap Ability):**  
  * *Proof Before Scale:* Spend **1 Catalytic R\&D Token** to pilot **`B:01` (Tamariki of the First 2000 Days — *Te Korowai Mokopuna* / *Whānau Ora*)** or **`B:02` / `C:03` (*Tuakana–Teina* & Graeme Dingle Foundation Mentoring)** in one region at **50% cost**. Once piloted, the Crown can scale that card nationally on the next turn without paying "Untested Policy" friction penalties.  
* **Dual-Class / *Kotahitanga* Synergies:**  
  * *Paired with **`K:05` (The Pūrākau Weaver — Artist as Community Leader)**:* Turn statistical nodes into lived community trust. Double the uptake of early-years wraparound and adolescent mentoring (reaching **65.0%+** of Years 8–10) and make your cohort immune to **Central Bureaucracy Red Tape (`H:02`)**.  
  * *Paired with **`K:04` (The "1925 Moment" Architect — Philanthropist as Economist)**:* Anchor philanthropic evaluations in **cardinal adult outcomes** (earnings, mental health, avoided state care) rather than arbitrary test scores—instantly unlocking **`B:07` (ACC Actuarial Time-Bend)** and **`N:03` (Williamson's Actuarial Ledger)** for national scaling.  
* **Flavor Text:** *"Markets don't lend to three-year-olds, and bureaucracies can't manufacture trust. We bring the aroha, the patience, and the proof that makes national transformation undeniable."*

### `K:02` — The Enterprise Wayfinder: Venture Catalyst, Founder, & Growth Investor

* **Type:** Wayfinder Persona — Productive Capital & High-Wage Innovation (Lenses 2 & 3\)  
* **Innate *Kaupapa* (Tap Ability):**  
  * *Unchain the NZD 1.6T Transfer:* Whenever **`B:05` (Use-It-Or-Lose-It Rebalancer)** is active, rotate **1 Dormant Property Token** into **High-Growth Operating Firms**, lifting regional business wages by **\+3.2% to \+9.6%** while keeping active entrepreneurs **net welfare positive all the way up to NZD 100M in net worth**.  
* **Dual-Class / *Kotahitanga* Synergies:**  
  * *Paired with **`B:02` / Teacher & Mentor (Venture Catalyst as Teacher)**:* Pair the **Age-20 Youth Capital Grant (`B:03`)** with hands-on founder mentorship at Gate 2 (Ages 12–14) and Gate 3 (Ages 20–35). Cut the city brain drain to Australia from **19.3%–23.4% down to 7.6% Nat.** and turn first-generation graduates into domestic job creators.  
  * *Paired with **`K:03` (The Sovereign Helmsman)**:* Co-sign the **Corporate Tax Cut \+ Land/Estate Rebalance (`B:05`)**, proving to the business community that taxing dormant dirt instead of productive enterprise grows the pie for 95% of households.  
* **Flavor Text:** *"Hoarding residential land is how a country flounders in displacement mode. Backing retained young talent with patient capital is how a nation gets up on the wing-sail."*

### `K:03` — The Sovereign Helmsman: Policy-Maker, Minister, Treasury, & Reserve Bank Official

* **Type:** Wayfinder Persona — Actuarial Governance & Macro Stability (All 3 Lenses)  
* **Innate *Kaupapa* (Tap Ability):**  
  * *Bridge the OIA 20250878 Gap:* Play **`B:08` (The 1925 Quantum Leap)** to couple micro-cohort social outcomes directly to the Crown's macroeconomic Long-Term Fiscal Model (`LTFM`). Book **3.84x Verified Actuarial Breakeven by Year 5** (1.16x from direct cost avoidance alone via **`B:07`**) and engage **`B:06` (The 2050 50/50 Stabilizer)**.  
* **Dual-Class / *Kotahitanga* Synergies:**  
  * *Paired with **`K:01` (The Catalytic Steward)**:* Adopt **`N:04` (Ostrom's Polycentric Commons)**—funding proven community wraparound at national scale while slashing **NZD 17.7B–28.5B/yr** in reactive Oranga Tamariki, hospital, and justice hull drag down to **NZD 4.2B/yr (`B:04`)**.  
  * *Paired with **`K:04` (The "1925 Moment" Architect)**:* Replace blunt OCR monetary hammering and reactive austerity with **Energy & Housing Firming (`C:04`, `C:05`)**, cutting domestic interest rates by **60 bps**, keeping **Universal NZ Super at Age 65**, and delivering a **\-3.5 percentage-point PAYE tax cut** in `SS2`.  
* **Flavor Text:** *"For forty years, our rulebook forced us to count investment in children as debt and infrastructure neglect as thrift. Give us the right actuarial compass, and we will steer Aotearoa into clear air."*

### `K:04` — The "1925 Moment" Architect: Quantitative Economist & Open-Source Researcher

* **Type:** Wayfinder Persona — *A System of Profound Knowledge* & GPU Proof (System-Wide)  
* **Innate *Kaupapa* (Tap Ability):**  
  * *Inspect the 8-PType Engine:* Reveal the hidden general-equilibrium feedback loops across **Rural, Small, Large, Auckland, and Abroad (`C:05`)**. Neutralize any "Ideological Bluff" or "Zero-Sum Myth" card played by the Orthodoxy Deck (`H:01` – `H:05`) by citing empirical Dunedin Study elasticities and the **11 Historical Precedents**.  
* **Dual-Class / *Kotahitanga* Synergies:**  
  * *Paired with **`K:02` (The Enterprise Wayfinder — Founder as Economist)**:* Combine **`N:01` (Coase)**, **`N:03` (Williamson)**, **`N:04` (Ostrom)**, and **`N:05` (Romer)** to prove that lowering transaction costs and unlocking non-rival human capability generates a **\+20.49% National Welfare Gain** and **NZD 1,510.7B in 60-year cash GDP (`B:08`, `B:09`)**.  
  * *Paired with **`K:06` (The Kaumātua & Founder's Dividend)**:* Synthesize 60 years of lived entrepreneurial and civic history into open-source code and interactive Sankey diagrams—reducing the deployment cost of all **Phase 1 (`SS1`)** and **Phase 2 (`SS2`)** reforms by **100%**.  
* **Flavor Text:** *"In 1925, physics had all the math on the shelf to leap from classical drag to quantum mechanics in three years. Macroeconomics stands at that exact threshold today."*

### `K:05` — The *Pūrākau* Weaver: Creative, Storyteller, & Cultural Catalyst

* **Type:** Wayfinder Persona — Narrative Imagination & National Renaissance (Gates 2 & 3\)  
* **Innate *Kaupapa* (Tap Ability):**  
  * *Shatter the Myth of Managed Decline:* Transform the 17 nodes of the Population Sankey and the 26 nodes of the Fiscal Ledger into a living story where every Kiwi sees their own *whānau* and *mokopuna*. Remove all **"Political Paralysis"** and **"Tall Poppy Cynicism"** counters from the board.  
* **Dual-Class / *Kotahitanga* Synergies:**  
  * *Paired with **`K:01` (The Catalytic Steward)**:* Infuse Gate 2 adolescent mentoring (`B:02`, `C:03`) with cultural identity (*Ako*, *Tuakana–Teina*, creative arts), lifting thriving youth launch from **23.5% to 65.0%** and raising **`C:06` (Social Credit Rating / Cohesion Index) from 100 to 126**.  
  * *Paired with **`K:02` (The Enterprise Wayfinder)**:* Combine **`B:03` (Age-20 Renaissance Founder)** and cooled urban rents (`B:05`) to ignite a **Domestic Creative & Tech Renaissance**—turning Wellington, Auckland, Christchurch, and provincial hubs into global magnets for talent.  
* **Flavor Text:** *"Equations and actuarial tables give us permission to fly, but nobody boards the vessel without a story worth believing in. Building the future is ultimately a creative act."*

### `K:06` — The *Kaumātua* & Founder's Dividend: Wisdom of the Golden Years (Lens 3 — Ages 65+)

* **Type:** Wayfinder Lifecycle Identity — Intergenerational Wisdom & Catalytic Bequest (Continuous)  
* **Requirement:** Keep **Universal NZ Superannuation Solvent at Age 65 (`B:06`)** \+ Retain Domestic Youth (`SS1 -> SS2`)  
* **Innate *Kaupapa* (Tap Ability):**  
  * *Freedom from Survival Mode:* Because Universal NZ Superannuation is secured at Age 65 and energy/living costs are firmed, retiring cohorts do not have to spend their golden years scrambling against poverty or mourning exiled grandchildren.  
  * *The 60-Year Synthesis (Non-Rival Wisdom):* Tap a thriving Age 65+ Persona to contribute **1 Lifetime Experience Token** every turn at zero fiscal cost. Spend this token to:  
    1. **Architect a Paradigm Shift:** Reduce the cost of any **Nobel Milestone (`N:01` – `N:05`)** or **Systems Innovation (`B:04`, `B:08`)** card by **100%** (representing open-source monographs, policy R\&D, and institutional mentorship that only a lifetime of experience can produce), OR  
    2. **Intergenerational *Tuakana–Teina* & Angel Seed:** Pair directly with **Gate 2 (`B:02`, Ages 12–14)** or **Gate 3 (`B:03`, Ages 20–35)** to double the startup survival rate of first-generation founders and boost the **Social Cohesion Index from 100 to 126 (`C:06`)**.  
* **Flavor Text:** *"It takes sixty years of scraped knees, contrarian bets, and lived history to see how the whole board fits together. Secure dignity in retirement, and a nation's elders become its greatest open-source library."*

### `K:07` — *Te Tiriti* & The Big Table Crew: The Five-Crew Hydrofoil Formation

* **Type:** Legendary *Kaupapa* Enchantment — Multi-Persona Crew Synergy  
* **Requirement:** At least three Dual-Class Wayfinder Personas (`K:01` – `K:06`) active at the table (representing Crown *Kāwanatanga*, Iwi/Community *Rangatiratanga*, Enterprise, Science, and Story).  
* **Game Text:**  
  * *One Crew, One Foiling Vessel:* On Emirates Team New Zealand's catamaran, if the naval architect, the wing trimmer, and the helmsman pull in different directions, the boat pitch-poles. While **The Big Table Crew (`K:07`)** is active, every Persona at the table shares its **Innate *Kaupapa* Ability** with all other players.  
  * *Prosperity We Can Keep:* Restore New Zealand's **Transparency International Social Credit Rating (`C:06`)** to **\#1 in the World**, permanently lock in **`B:06` (The 2050 50/50 Stabilizer)**, and unlock **`B:09` (Lifting the Gold from the River)** and **`N:06` (The Aotearoa Synthesis)**.  
* **Flavor Text:** *"We have found our new gold. We are standing in the river. When all five crews lift together, Aotearoa doesn't just survive the heavy seas—she flies."*
