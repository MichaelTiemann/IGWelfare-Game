### **How This Plays in a Classroom or Workshop (The "3-Turn Demo")**

To as a 20-minute interactive tabletop or browser game with students, young voters, or policy officials, play these three rounds:

1. **Round 1 (Displacement Mode / `SS0`):** Players try to survive using only conventional tools (raising PAYE taxes, cutting infrastructure maintenance, or relying on high-churn replacement migration). They watch the **Oranga Tamariki / Hospital / Justice drag** eat their budget while debt spirals toward 200% of GDP.  
2. **Round 2 (Single-Lens Pitch-Pole):** Players try playing *only* early-childhood spending without taxing land wealth or changing Treasury's cash-flow accounting (the **Scotland scenario**), or *only* industrial growth without land-value recycling (the **1989 Tokyo scenario**). They see firsthand why the boat nose-dives when one foil is missing.  
3. **Round 3 (Up on Foils / `SS0 -> SS1 -> SS2`):** Players link the Nobel Laureate cards with the five Aotearoa levers—unlocking the Year 5 actuarial breakeven, surviving the Orthodoxy Hazards, and landing the `SS2` PAYE tax cut and infrastructure harvest.
