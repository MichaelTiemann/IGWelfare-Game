## 1. Welcome Aboard: Why We Play on Cardboard Before Cabinet

In the 2013 America's Cup, conventional boatbuilders assumed forty tons of displacement hull had to plow through heavy water. Emirates Team New Zealand read the exact same rulebook, curved the daggerboard into an **L-shaped hydrofoil**, coupled it to a rear **T-foil rudder stabilizer**, and lifted the hull into clear air.

Centuries earlier, Polynesian navigators solved an even harder systems problem across Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa: lashing **two hulls together with *kīato* cross-beams (*Te Waka Hourua*)** so neither hull could capsize in the Southern Ocean.

**Foils & Founders: The Aotearoa Deck** puts you at the helm of New Zealand's intergenerational political economy across **20 turns (`3 years per turn = 60 years`, 2026–2086)**:
* **Under Status Quo Drift (`SS0` — Displacement Mode):** `22%–30%` of children hit an early cash-subsistence corner, `3.10x` urban housing costs crowd out young families and educators, `1 in 5` metropolitan graduates depart across the Tasman, `61%` of retained top talent gets siphoned into passive land speculation (`z_l`), Crown Debt spirals toward `185%–200%` of GDP, PAYE climbs past `28.2%`, and national welfare sinks **`-16.21% CE`**.
* **Up on Coupled Hydrofoils (`SS1 -> SS2`):** By uniting early human capability, regional food-medicine & community hubs, fair land-to-enterprise tax rotation, and ACC-style forward actuarial accounting, **every single wealth vigintile (`1–20`) and all 4 regions win** (`+24.70% CE` pre-NDC / `+25.19% CE` on the 13-wave *Te Kāhui Waka* ledger), Crown Debt sinks to **`21%–22%` of GDP**, working-age PAYE drops from `22.0%` to **`18.25%`**, and Universal NZ Superannuation is **lowered from Age 65 to Age 64**!

## 4. Lifecycle Rules: The 3-Slot *Whānau* Mat & Biological Gates (`+3 Yrs/Turn`)

At the start of every turn (**Phase A — Sunrise**), advance every character on all *Whānau* Mats by **`+3 years`** and resolve any biological threshold crossed that turn:

### 4.1 The Five Lifecycle Thresholds ("Use-It-Or-Lose-It")
1. **Crossing Age 6 (`Slot 1` Gate 1 — End of the First 2000 Days):**
   * *Covered* (by an active `B:01` regional wave, a `2-Wealth` private bridge, or a neighbor's `K:09` *Aunty's House* shield): Place a **Readiness Counter (`N:03`)** on the child (`3x` skill multiplier at Gate 2–3!).
   * *Uncovered:* If Birth Vigintile is `1–4` (`1–6` when `H:03` *GUiNZ Drift* is active), attach a **Struggling Trajectory Token (`h1–h2`)** *and* add **+1 Contingent Liability** to the Crown Debt Track (`Nodes 22–25` future hospital/care/justice drag).
2. **Crossing Age 15 (`Slot 1` Gate 2–3 — The $\varphi_2 = -0.95$ Adolescent Lock):**
   * *Mentored* (by `B:03` *Tuakana–Teina* or `B:04` *GrowingChange*): Locks in **Wedge 1**, promotes the youth to **Thriving (`h6–h7`)**, grants shock immunity (`\Psi_{J=5}`), and stamps **Eligible for `B:05` Vanguard Grant**!
   * *Unmentored:* Any unbanked `B:01` Readiness Counter suffers **Skill Fade-Out** (`22.0%` 1-term loss, or `39.2%` 2-term loss under `T:07`!).
3. **Crossing Age 20 (`Slot 1 -> Slot 2` Gate 4 — Adult Launch & Brain-Drain Check):**
   * Move the character into `Slot 2` (*Pakeke* Working Adult: earns `1 Wealth/turn` if Struggling/`z_l`-squeezed, `2 Wealth/turn` if Core Trades, `3 Wealth/turn` if Thriving `z_h` Builder).
   * *Seeded* (via `B:05` Vanguard Grant in Turns `1–2`, or universal `B:06` once active): Clears the **`20%` LVR Deposit Cliff** (when paired with `B:08`), stamps a **Durable Equity Seal (`E:05`, repeal-proof!)**, and grants **Brain-Drain Immunity**.
   * *Unseeded in an Uncooled Metro (`Large` / `Auckland`):* Roll `1d20` against **`H:04` (*Brain-Drain Vortex*)**—on **`1–5`**, exile that `Slot 2` adult to the **Sydney/Abroad Zone** until repatriated by `B:06` + `B:08`!
4. **Crossing Age 65 (`SS0/SS1`) or Age 64 (`SS2` via `B:15`) (`Slot 2 -> Slot 3` — Retirement):**
   * Move into `Slot 3` (*Kaumātua/Kuia*): activates **`K:09`** (`K_whanau` Pool access + **`1 free Experience Token/turn`** that pays `100%` of any `N:01–N:08`, `B:10`, or `E:02` cost!).
5. **Crossing Age 90 (`Slot 3 -> Slot 1` — Estate Settlement & Rebirth):**
   * Settle estate (`B:07` CGT/Estate recycle or `3x` `E:02` Warm-Glow Solar Bequest), then roll **`B:00`** for a newborn (`Age 0`) entering `Slot 1`! *(If an `E:05` Wave-Echo cohort from ~7 turns earlier is now parenting, the newborn starts with a free Readiness Counter!)*

### 4.2 `2-of-3` Asymmetric *Whānau* Mode vs. Endogenous 3rd-Slot Unlock
* In **Standard Coopetitive Play (`3–6 Players`)**, each player starts with **only 2 occupied generational slots** (`1 empty slot`). Because you lack either a Child (`Slot 1`), a Cash Earner (`Slot 2`), or an Elder (`Slot 3`), you **must trade across the River** (`Variant B` Royalties) to cover your missing generation!
* **Unlocking Your 3rd Slot Mid-Game:** As soon as the table activates **`B:15` (Super Age 64) + `N:08` (Fertility Bargaining `TFR -> 1.87 / 2.03`)**—or repatriates an exiled adult via **`B:06` + `B:08`**—every player may immediately populate their empty 3rd slot!

## 7. Victory, Endgame Scoring (`Variant B`) & Game Modes

The game ends at the close of **Turn 20 (`Year 60`)**—or **immediately in Mythic Generational Victory** if the table satisfies **`B:16` (*Lifting the Gold from the River*)**: Struggling Launch $\le 1.5\%$, Social Cohesion at `126.0` (`C:02`), and `B:14` + `B:15` active at `100%` Sufficiency!

### 7.1 Final Coopetitive Stewardship Score (`Variant B`)
$$\mathrm{Score}_i = \Bigl(\mathrm{Mana}_{\text{Zone A},i} + \mathrm{Royalties}_{i} + \mathrm{CohortTierBonus}_{i} + \left\lfloor \frac{\mathrm{Wealth}_{i}}{5} \right\rfloor - \mathrm{BystanderPenalties}_{i}\Bigr) \times \mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{Regime}}$$

* **Shared Hull Regime Multiplier ($\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{Regime}}$):**
  * **Sunk in `SS0` LTFM Drift (`-16.21% CE` / Debt $>100\%$ / Struggling $>15\%$):** **`0.5x` — Table Defeat!** *(Nobody wins on a capsized waka; Orthodoxy wins).*
  * **Stable `SS1` Foil-Borne (`+15.04% CE` National):** **`1.0x`**.
  * **Full `SS2` Harvest (`+24.70% CE` Pre-NDC, Debt `21%–22%`, `18.25%` PAYE, Super Age `64`):** **`1.5x` + `0.05x` per Turn Early** (`1.75x` under Turn-5 `Plan C`! Add **`+0.25x` Resilience Bonus** if the crew absorbed **`H:09` `3.0%` GDP Defense Shock** at `+21.18% CE`!).
* **Cohort Tier Bonus (Per Active *Whānau* Slot at Game End):** `+3 Mana` per **Thriving (`h6–h7` / `z_h`)** slot, `+1 Mana` per **Core (`h3–h5`)** slot, `-2 Mana` per unrescued **Struggling (`h1–h2`)** or exiled **Sydney** slot.

Whenever another player taps a card you founded (or your `K:09` *Aunty's House* shield), the Commons mints **`+2 Mana`** to you for specialist/provincial cards (`B:05`, `C:08–C:10`, `E:02`) or **`+1 Mana`** for central hub cards (`B:01`, `B:08`), up to `2` payouts/card/turn (`3` with `N:06`).
* **Free-Rider / Bystander Forfeit:** `0` Zone-B Mana staked in an era (`SS1` or `SS2`) incurs **`-4 Mana`**.
