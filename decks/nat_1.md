# Confirming the Fumble: The "Nat 1" Sub-Roll (`01:01` to `01:20`)
*Companion to `B:00` (*Te Tatau o Rangiātea — The Rawlsian D20*), `cards.yaml` (`v2026.11`), & `Documents 1A, 2A, 2B, 3`*

Rolling a **Natural 1** on `B:00` in tabletop RPGs is a "Critical Fail"—you are born into the **Bottom 5% of New Zealand household wealth (`P0.0` to `P5.0`, Tier 1)**, where net worth is zero or negative (overdrafts, payday loans, emergency housing debt) and family income sits at or below the `NZD 45k–50k` severe material hardship threshold (`Node 1` DEP-17 `9+`).

If you roll that **Nat 1** and then **roll the D20 a second time** ("confirming the fumble," where each face `01:01` through `01:20` represents a **0.25% slice of the birth cohort** from the deepest acute state-care crisis at `P0.00–0.25` up to the working-poor rental threshold at `P4.75–5.00`), the contrast between Status Quo Drift (`SS0`) and Coupled Foil-Borne Reform (`SS1 -> SS2`) is the moral and fiscal heart of the entire monograph:

1. **In the Status Quo (`SS0` — Displacement Mode, `-16.21% CE` LTFM Drift):** **All 20 faces (`01:01` to `01:20`) are trapped in the 100% Unmitigated Cash-Subsistence Corner (`i_c = 0`).** Because `3.10x` Auckland and `2.05x` Large-city rents consume over `50%–60%` of income (or `2.74x` rural fuel/transport eats the budget), parents have zero cash left for early childhood enrichment ($\bar{g}_{\mathrm{ECE}} = 0.032$). Worse, rolling `01:01` to `01:08` lands the child directly in the acute **Exits 1, 2, and 4** pipeline (`Nodes 22, 23, 25`)—Oranga Tamariki residential/custody care (**NZD 37.1k–54.7k/yr** shared care up to **NZD 180k/yr** acute residential), substantiated abuse in care (**NZD 181k** discounted lifetime Crown fiscal cost + **NZD 800k+** human harm), avoidable paediatric hospitalisations, and adult Corrections (**NZD 150k/prisoner-yr**)—imposing an average burden of **NZD 105,000 per acute-hardship child per year** while exposing the child to a **52%–92% probability of launching into adulthood on a struggling trajectory (`h1–h2`, reduced or lacking foundational skills)**.
2. **In the IGWelfare Scenario (`SS0 -> SS1 -> SS2` — Up on Hydrofoils, `+24.70% CE` National Pre-NDC):** **Every single face on the second die (`01:01` to `01:20`) is rescued from the `i_c = 0` corner and achieves a `+23.5%` to `+38.4%` permanent lifetime welfare gain.** By bypassing the household's cash constraint with **NZD 13.0k/yr of universal in-kind First-2000-Days wraparound (`B:01`) + *Te Māra Kai* Farm-to-ECE nutrition (`B:02`)**, buffering adolescent shocks at ages 12–14 (`\varphi_2 = -0.95`) with **Montessori / *Tuakana–Teina*, *GrowingChange*, and Graeme Dingle Foundation mentoring (`B:03`, `B:04`, `C:04`)**, compressing energy/rent floors (`E:01`, `B:08`), and seeding every mentored 20-year-old with the **Youth Capital Grant (`B:05` Vanguard / `B:06` Universal, `NZD 62k–76k` equity seed)**, **19 out of 20 faces (`01:02` to `01:20`) exit the struggling launch tier overwhelmingly**, and even the most complex acute-needs slice (`01:01`) sees state-care entries fall by over **94%** (from `5,500` down to under `300` nationally).
