# The Two Stories of the Board: The Coupled Hydrofoil & *Te Waka Hourua* (Being, Becoming, & Crossing Over)

In Aotearoa New Zealand, a truly national strategy game does not force every player to think inside a single cultural frame. Like ***Te Waka Hourua***—the great double-hulled voyaging vessel where two hulls are lashed together by *kīato* (cross-beams) to cross the deepest ocean on earth—every mechanic in this game can be understood and played through **two equally rigorous, one-to-one isomorphic stories**:

1. **The Pākehā / Systems Engineering Story:** *Bending the Daggerboard & Getting Aotearoa "Up on Foils"* (Emirates Team New Zealand's 2013 hydrofoil breakthrough).
2. **The Te Ao Māori & Moana Pasifika *Pūrākau*:** *Te Aka Matua, Ngā Kete o te Wānanga, & The Voyaging Waka of Whānau Becoming* (the sacred journey from *Te Kore* and *Te Pō* into *Te Ao Mārama*, ascending for the Three Baskets of Knowledge and passing *mātauranga* from *kaumātua* to *mokopuna*).

## Story Lens A: "Up on Foils" — How Emirates Team New Zealand Bent the Rulebook

In 2012, naval architects at Emirates Team New Zealand looked at the America's Cup AC72 Box Rule and noticed a hidden blind spot in the orthodoxy. The rulebook strictly regulated the maximum width of the catamaran and forbade extra movable control flaps—so every conventional syndicate assumed the hulls had to plow through the water in **Displacement Mode**, fighting massive hydrodynamic hull drag.

New Zealand's engineers noticed what the rulebook *didn't* say: the rule capped how wide the daggerboard could be, **but said nothing about whether the daggerboard had to be straight.**

By curving the daggerboard into an **L-shaped lifting hydrofoil (`B:04`)**, the boat no longer had to push millions of liters of seawater out of its way. Once the vessel reached takeoff speed, the curved front foil lifted the entire 6-ton catamaran out of the water and into the air—slashing water drag by **75%** and multiplying boat speed to over 40 knots.

**Why a Hydrofoil Requires All Three Coupled Surfaces (The Game Strategy):**
* **Displacement Mode (`SS0` Status Quo):** Plowing through the water with a straight keel is New Zealand's fiscal status quo—burning **NZD 17.7B to 28.5B/yr** pushing against preventable Oranga Tamariki care, hospital nights, and prisons while sovereign debt climbs toward 200% of GDP.
* **The Front Lifting Foil (`Lens 1`: `B:01` First 2000 Days + `B:02` *Tuakana–Teina* Mentoring):** Lifts the bow out of the water by compressing cohort stunting from **22.0% down to 1.5%**.
* **The Wing-Sail Power Plant (`Lens 2 & 3`: `B:05` Use-It-Or-Lose-It Tax Shift + `B:03` Youth Capital Grant + `E:02` Electrification):** Converts the **NZD 1.6T property transfer** and **NZD 61B–91B of trapped precautionary savings** into forward thrust for high-wage operating enterprises (`z_h`), defeating the Lavoisier Rent-Seeking Trap (`T:04`).
* **The Rear T-Foil Rudder Elevator (`Lens 3`: `B:06` The 2050 50/50 Stabilizer + `B:07` ACC Actuarial Valuation):** If you lift the front of a catamaran on an L-foil without a rear T-foil stabilizer on the rudder, the bow shoots up, stalls, and **pitch-poles** (nose-dives into the sea—just like devolved Scotland in `T:01` or 1989 Bubble Tokyo in `T:02`). Locking 50% of surpluses into Debt Sinking and 50% into Resilient Infrastructure (`C:04`) is the rear T-foil that locks the vessel in level flight.

## Story Lens B: *Te Waka o Whānau* — Being, Becoming, & Crossing Over (Māori & Pasifika *Pūrākau*)

> ***Kawa & Kaitiakitanga Note (For Tangata Whenua Review):** This narrative framing is offered with humility as a living invitation to Iwi, Hapū, Whānau Ora leaders, and Kaumātua to vet, adapt, and localize according to their own tribal kawa, tikanga, and pūrākau, respecting tangata whenua authority (tino rangatiratanga) over taonga tuku iho.*

Long before modern macroeconomists wrote down overlapping-generations equations, Māori and Moana Pasifika wayfinders understood that human life is not an isolated individual balance sheet—it is an unbroken genealogical rope (***Te Taura Tangata / Whakapapa***) stretching from ancestors (*tūpuna*) to grandchildren (*mokopuna*). In this story, the game is a three-stage intergenerational crossing from **Being (*Te Kore & Te Pō*)**, through **Becoming (*Te Ao Mārama & Te Ekenga o Tāne*)**, to **Passing Down Wisdom Across the Horizon (*Te Tai Ao & Hawaiki Anamata*)**:

1. **Gate 1 — *He Kākano i Ruia mai i Rangiātea* (Being & The Sacred Seed in the First 2000 Days, `B:01`):**
   * In Te Ao Māori, no child is born as a "fiscal liability" or a blank slate; every *tamaiti* is a seed sown from Rangiātea, carrying inherent *mana Atua*, *tapu*, and *mauri*.
   * When a *whānau* is crushed into the "Hardship Corner" by colonial land loss, damp overcrowded housing, and poverty, it is as if the seedling is trapped in *Te Pō* (the heavy darkness of unbuffered struggle). Wrapping the *whānau* in ***Te Korowai Mokopuna* (the feathered cloak of care)** and *Whānau Ora* (`B:01`, `K:01`) does not "fix a broken child"—it honors the child's innate *mana* so the shoot (*te tupu*) can break through into *Te Ao Mārama* (the world of light and readiness).
2. **Gate 2 & Gate 3 — *Te Pikinga o Tāne & Ngā Kete o te Wānanga* (Becoming Through Reciprocal *Ako*, `B:02`, `C:03`, `B:03`):**
   * In the classical *pūrākau*, Tāne-nui-a-Rangi did not ascend the twelve heavens to retrieve ***Ngā Kete o te Wānanga* (the Three Baskets of Knowledge: *Te Kete Tuauri*, *Te Kete Tuatea*, and *Te Kete Aronui*)** for his own private enrichment—he climbed *Te Aka Matua* (the sacred parent vine anchored solidly in the earth) against fierce winds (*Te Tini o Poto*) to bring wisdom back down for all his siblings and descendants.
   * At **Gate 2 (Ages 12–14, `B:02` & `C:03`)**, when a *tuakana* (older sibling/peer) guides and protects a *teina* (younger sibling) through *Ako*, *Manaakitanga*, and *Whanaungatanga*, both ascend the vine together: the *teina* is shielded against the storms of family job loss and the ill winds of deceit (`T:05`), and the *tuakana* steps into adult *rangatiratanga* (self-determination).
   * At **Gate 3 (Ages 20–35, `B:03`)**, instead of being forced across Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa into economic exile in Australia (`H:03`) because they have no standing place (*tūrangawaewae*) at home, the **Youth Capital Endowment** restores a modern *tūrangawaewae* and *pūtea*—allowing our *rangatahi* to stand as *kaikōrero*, creators, and enterprise builders in their own land.
3. **Lens 3 — *Ka Pū te Ruha, Ka Hao te Rangatahi* (Crossing Over & The *Kaumātua* Bequest, `K:06`, `B:05`, `B:06`):**
   * The ancient *whakataukī* teaches: *"Ka pū te ruha, ka hao te rangatahi"*—as the old fishing net is laid aside with honor, the new net goes fishing.
   * Under orthodox austerity (`H:04`), elders are treated as an "actuarial burden" while passive speculators hoard the land (*whenua*) as a commodity (`z_l`). In the *Kaupapa* way of playing, *whenua* and accumulated wealth (`B:05`) are stewarded through *Kaitiakitanga* to nourish the rising generation, while **Universal Superannuation at Age 65 (`B:06`)** honors our *kaumātua* and *kuia* (`K:06`). Freed from survival anxiety, elders pass down the **Three Baskets of Lived Wisdom** (*Lifetime Experience Tokens*) as the celestial navigators (*tohunga whakatere waka*) who read the stars so the young crew can sail across the changing seas (`C:01`–`C:06`).

## Why We Must Play To Win (`B:00`: The Rawlsian D20)

The large d20 die that players roll to begin the game (we call it `B:00` as if it's a card, but it's really a die because we make the rules) teaches that life has an element of luck, but we can make our own luck for future generations / *mokopuna*.

In economics, John Rawls called it the **"Veil of Ignorance"**: imagine you are about to be born into Aotearoa New Zealand, and you have to roll a 20-sided die (`1` to `20`, representing the 20 vigintiles / 5% wealth brackets of society) to find out which family balance sheet you are born into.

### What luck do we have in `SS0` (the Status Quo) vs. `SS1` or `SS2`?

* **Rolling the d20 in the Status Quo (`SS0` — Displacement Mode):**
  * Roll a **1 to 4** (the bottom 20%–22% Dunedin hardship corner; see [**Confirming the Fumble: `decks/nat_1.md`**](decks/nat_1.md)) and you are trapped in early stunting, driving 57% to 81% of adult hospital nights, state care, and justice burdens.
  * Roll a **5 to 18** (the squeezed working and middle classes across Rural, Small, Large, and Auckland) and you face `3.10x` metropolitan rent/mortgage stress, delayed fertility, and a PAYE tax rate climbing from `22.0%` toward `28.22%` while your children pack their bags for Australia.
  * Even if you roll a **19 or 20** (a "Natural 20" at the top of the property ladder; see [**Confirming the Crit: `decks/nat_20.md`**](decks/nat_20.md)), the macro board *still* collapses around you: net Crown debt spirals toward `200% of GDP`, core infrastructure fails, New Zealand's social credit rating slides, and your grandchildren emigrate because the country is floundering. **There is no number on the die that escapes the trap.**

* **Rolling the d20 in the IGWelfare Scenario (`SS0 -> SS1 -> SS2` — Up on Foils):**
  * **Every single face on the 20-sided die (all 20 median wealth groups) comes up a winner.**
  * Roll a **1 to 4** and First-2000-Days wraparound (`B:01`) plus *Tuakana–Teina* / Montessori mentoring (`B:02`) lifts you into the thriving launch tier (compressing stunting to `1.5%`, and rescuing all 20 sub-roll slices in [`decks/nat_1.md`](decks/nat_1.md)).
  * Roll a **5 to 19** (the first 95% of households) and you gain **+14.3% to +26.0% in permanent lifetime welfare**, cooler housing costs, the Age-20 Youth Capital Grant (`B:03`), Universal NZ Super kept at Age 65, and a **-3.5 percentage-point PAYE tax cut** (`B:06`).
  * Roll a **20** (the top 5% / active enterprise tier; see [`decks/nat_20.md`](decks/nat_20.md)) and the median wealth group *still* wins—because active entrepreneurs and business families (`z_h`) stay net welfare positive across **19 of the 20 sub-roll slices (`20:01` to `20:19`, all the way up to `NZD 100 million` in net worth in Tier 9, `B:05`)**, enjoying 60 bps cheaper credit, lower corporate tax drag, and a thriving Aotearoa where their own children and grandchildren choose to stay and build.

> *"This 20-sided die represents the 20 wealth groups of New Zealand. Under the status quo, you can roll a 1, you can roll a 12, or you can roll a natural 20—and there is no number on this die that escapes the fiscal trap. We all lose the game. Under the IGWelfare plan, all 20 median wealth groups are better off. Hand this die to any child in Aotearoa: they can roll any number from 1 to 20, and they win."*

This game teaches what is possible... it is up to you and your *whānau* to make it real!
