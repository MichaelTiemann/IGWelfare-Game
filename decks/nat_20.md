## Three Takeaways From the "Double-20" Roll (Why This Stuns the Room)

1. **The "19-Out-of-20 Win, 20th Breaks Even" Builder Rule (`B:08` + `K:05`):** Under Guvenen et al.'s (2023) *"Use-It-Or-Lose-It"* capital reallocation (`B:08`), active `z_h` entrepreneurs (`omega_ent = 0.80`) gain **`+5.6%` down to `+1.2% CE`** all the way through `20:19` (**IRD High-Wealth Median: `NZD 2.1M income + NZD 50M net worth`**), and break even (`-0.1% CE`) at `20:20` (**NBR Rich List Median: `NZD 6.5M + 140M`**)—because cutting corporate tax from `20%` to `12%`, cutting PAYE to `18.25%` (`B:15` + `C:10`), lowering credit spreads `-60 to -100 bps` (`B:14` + `E:03`), and expanding the thriving `h6–h7` domestic talent pool from `23.5%` to `65.0%` (`Wedge 3 = +5.20% CE`) outweigh the `1.5%` book-value land/wealth levy!
2. **Two-Thirds of the Top 5% (`20:01` to `20:13`) Win Even Before Portfolio Rotation:** In New Zealand, `P95.0` to `P98.25` (`NZD 3.0M` to `NZD 6.0M` net worth) consists overwhelmingly of senior clinicians, partners, primary-sector operators, and SME founders whose household gains from `18.25%` PAYE, cheaper business/mortgage credit, and retained children/grandchildren keep even their **blended tier-average welfare positive (`+2.3%` to `0.0% CE`)**.
3. **The `20:20` Sovereign Insurance Bargain (`95.3%–98.5%` Retained vs. `185%` Crown Debt):** Even on `20:20`, an active family enterprise retains **`95.3%` of its fortune (`NZD 133.4M` of `140M`)** and a passive land dynasty retains **`90.2%` (`NZD 126.3M`)**—a far smaller adjustment than routine once-a-decade macro drawdowns (`-60%` in 1987 equities, `-40%` in the 2008 GFC, or `-22% to -27%` in the 2021–2024 Auckland/Wellington property drop), while trading a `-16.21% CE` / `185%`-debt fiscal sinkhole for `#1` global institutional trust (`126.0`, `C:02`) and grandchildren who build their lives in Aotearoa instead of Sydney.
